# Data

Signals for the numbers a team cares about, with their history, sources and relationships in one place. Most other recipes build on it for charts, signal pickers and saved board views.

## Highlights

- Signals with history, owners and dependency edges
- Built-in pulls for GitHub, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, npm, blog RSS, Hacker News and Substack
- Data sources that sync provider measures into bound signals, plus hand entry
- Circuit boards that lay out signals (and, with planning, goals and initiatives) and draw what moves what
- Presets: named saved views over the boards other recipes provide
