# Planning

Goals that put a target and a window on a signal, initiatives that say how the team will move it, broken into tasks and scheduled into sprints with real per-person capacity. Long-form rationale stays in markdown next to the structured state.

## Highlights

- Goals: one signal, a target and a window, measured against the signal's history with a health reading (on track, at risk, off track, met)
- Cycles that group goals into a period, with a review step to record outcomes and carry open goals forward
- Initiatives with kind, status, value and priority ratings, dependencies and the goals they contribute to
- Tasks sized in whole hours, grouped under initiatives or owned by a sprint alone
- Sprints with per-person availability, a planning grid and a daily stand-up board
- Rationale docs on disk, with row summaries derived from each doc
- Configurable initiative kinds

## Builds on

Core only: goals measure core's signals, and saved views are core. Placing goals and initiatives on boards, and laying a cycle out as one, is the `cycle-board` powerup.
