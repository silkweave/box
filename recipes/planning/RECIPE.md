# Planning

Initiatives tied to the signals they are meant to move, broken into tasks and scheduled into sprints with real per-person capacity. Long-form rationale stays in markdown next to the structured state.

## Highlights

- Initiatives with kind, status, value and priority ratings, dependencies and bound signals
- Tasks sized in whole hours, grouped under initiatives or owned by a sprint alone
- Sprints with per-person availability, a planning grid and a daily stand-up board
- Rationale docs on disk, with row summaries derived from each doc
- Configurable initiative kinds

## Mobile

- A Plan tab in the Box mobile app with initiatives by status and your open tasks across them
- Initiative and task pages where every field can be changed in place, plus create, rename, move and delete
- Initiative and task docs rendered as markdown, with an editor
