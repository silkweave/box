# Planning

Initiatives that hold the work and tasks that do it, nested as deep as the work goes, sized in hours and tracked to done. Long-form rationale stays in markdown next to the structured state, and goals, milestones, sprints and a timeline are powerups.

## Highlights

- Initiatives with kind, status, impact and effort (an impact x effort quadrant), priority, owner, tags, and start and due dates
- An initiatives board as a list or a status board, with saved views, filters and grouping
- Tasks nested under each other to any depth, sized in whole hours, grouped under initiatives or standalone, with server-assigned keys (`I1`, `T1`)
- An initiative's progress in hours from its tasks, and a `planning.tasks_done` measure any signal can bind
- An All tasks page: every task in one list, filtered by initiative (No initiative included), status and owner
- Task details open in a right panel beside the list (a push view on narrow screens)
- Rationale docs on disk, with row summaries derived from each doc
- Configurable initiative kinds

## Builds on

Core only: saved views and signals are core. Goals and cycles, milestones, sprints, the timeline, the cycle board and the mobile module are powerups in `powerups/`.
