# Cycle board

Planning's goals and initiatives on boards, and a planning cycle laid out as a board in one click: the cycle's goals on top, the signal each one measures beneath it, and the drivers of those signals at the bottom, with each goal's initiatives riding on its card.

## Highlights

- Goals and initiatives as board cards: a goal with its reading, target, pace and health, an initiative with its status and open work
- Placements follow when a goal is renamed, and leave when a goal or initiative is deleted
- A Circuit board action on every cycle page that has goals
- The board is arranged for you, bottom-up from what moves to what it moves
- Re-seed a cycle's board after the plan changes, with a confirmation before its arrangement is replaced
- An agent tool, `cycle-board`, that does the same
