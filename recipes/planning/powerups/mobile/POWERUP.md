# Mobile app

The Planning screens in the Box mobile app, as a module the app registers alongside the others.

## Highlights

- A Plan tab in the Box mobile app with initiatives by status and your open tasks across them
- Initiative and task pages where every field can be changed in place, plus create, move and delete
- Items nested as on the web: an initiative's tasks as a tree, and sub-tasks added under any item
- With the milestones powerup: milestones in the tree with their state, progress and health, an editable outcome flag, and initiative health
- With the sprints powerup: a task's sprint and day as a read-only fact
- Initiative and task docs rendered as markdown, with an editor

## Needs

- The Flutter SDK to build and test it
- Xcode or Android Studio for a device build
