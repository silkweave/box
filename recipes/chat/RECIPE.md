# Chat

Team chat with rooms, threads and direct messages, plus a seat for the Box's agent that answers when someone mentions it. Messages live in the Box's own chat store, separate from the warehouse.

## Highlights

- Channels, direct messages, threads, mentions, reactions and attachments
- Read pointers and a live feed across every open tab
- The system agent answers mentions in rooms, with approval cards for actions
- Its own SQLite store with an append-only migration chain and automatic pre-migration backups

## Mobile

- A Chat tab in the Box mobile app with an unread badge, channels and direct messages
- Threads, reactions, mentions, attachments from the camera or files, and the agent's live activity
- In-app notifications for messages that arrive while you are elsewhere in the app
