# Chat

Team chat with rooms, threads and direct messages, plus seats for the Box's agents that answer when someone mentions them. Messages live in the Box's database, in the chat schema.

## Highlights

- Channels, direct messages, threads, mentions, reactions and attachments
- Read pointers and a live feed across every open tab
- Open rooms for everyone with chat access, or members-only rooms with a member list
- Agents answer mentions from a seat in a room, only where every reader covers them, with approval cards for actions
- Room settings preview every membership and seat change for agent coverage
