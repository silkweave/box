# CRM

A lightweight pipeline worked one company at a time, where the account is the deal and its status carries the whole lifecycle. Contacts, meetings, revenue and conversations sit together on each account page.

## Highlights

- Accounts with a pipeline kanban and a totalling data grid over the same saved views
- Contacts, meetings with outcomes, invoice-level revenue events and an activity stream per account
- A markdown doc for each account
- An assign queue for imported rows that could not be matched to an account
- A machine write path for the team's own sync that never overwrites human judgement
