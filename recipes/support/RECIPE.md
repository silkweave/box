# Support

Every question, problem and complaint the team hears from its users, recorded once as a ticket: who asked, what it was about and where it stands, in one tickets view with the team's saved presets, as a list or a board. Next to the tickets, the help articles, FAQ answers and getting-started guides that answer them.

## Highlights

- Tickets logged by hand, or by an agent from a call or chat summary through the Support tools
- One tickets view built like Initiatives: five statuses (Pending, Active, Blocked, Done, Dropped), presets (Open, Mine, Unassigned, Late, Blocked, Closed, Everything), search, filters, grouping, sort and columns, as a grid or a board you drag cards across
- Requesters kept once per person, found again by email or phone however the number was typed, edited and merged right on the ticket
- A ticket age in working days, with a late flag past the team's threshold
- Help Articles: articles, FAQ answers and getting-started guides in one list, each drafted, published, flagged for update or retired, with images and videos, and linked to the tickets it answered
- Help Articles built like the tickets: saved presets (Drafts, Published, Needs update, Featured, Everything), a list or a board, and the entries you opened recently
- An agent skill that logs and updates tickets by the team's conventions

## Personal data

Requesters' names, email addresses and phone numbers, and ticket summaries that can name people.
Internal users only; nothing in the base recipe is public (the Help Center powerup publishes the published help articles). The contact form's submissions (name, email, message) are also recorded as core events; the owner accepts that. See [SPEC.md](SPEC.md#personal-data).
